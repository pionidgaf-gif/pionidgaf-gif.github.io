What we do

Content

Monthly scripted Reels, stories, filming on site and around the neighbourhood: schools, kindergartens, markets.

Ads

Instagram and Facebook ads. We check cost per inquiry weekly and move budget to the videos that work.

Sales team

A script for managers, a sheet logging every inquiry and response time, mystery-buyer checks twice a month.

Reporting

Every Monday: inquiries received, inquiries answered, office visits and cost per inquiry.

Two-week pilot

During the pilot we message and call your sales team as buyers, film about six Reels, launch ads and deliver a report with numbers. If you sign a three-month contract, the pilot fee counts toward the first month.

Monthly work from 22M UZS, depending on project scope. Ad budget is separate.
