FAQ

How much does social media marketing cost in Fergana?

The SMM retainer is 28M UZS per month: six specialists on a three-month contract. Work with property developers starts at 22M UZS per month. A two-week pilot costs 9.4M UZS.

Which cities do you cover?

The Fergana Valley: Fergana, Margilan, Kokand, Kuvasay, Namangan and Andijan.

Do you work with real estate developers?

Yes. We film the project, run ads and check the sales team's response time, office invitations and follow-up calls, then set up scripts and monitoring.

What happens during the two-week pilot?

We message and call your sales team as buyers, film about six Reels, launch ads and deliver a report with numbers. The pilot costs 9.4M UZS and counts toward the first month if you sign a three-month contract.

Is the ad budget included in the price?

No. You pay Meta directly for ads; we set up the campaigns and report cost per inquiry every week.

What is in the weekly report?

Inquiries first: how many came in, how many were answered and how fast, how many people visited the office. Then views, reach and cost per inquiry.

Which developers have you worked with?

We ran all of JBA Group's projects at once, and worked with Dream Residence, TXT Group, Crystal Avenue, Central Avenue, Sohil Bo'yi, Yaqinlar, Qadrdonlar and Rich House.
